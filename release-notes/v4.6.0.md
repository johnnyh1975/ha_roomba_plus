# Roomba+ 4.6.0

Built on **roombapy-prime 0.9.1**, unchanged from 4.5.4. Everything from
the betas [4.6.0b1](v4.6.0b1.md), [b2](v4.6.0b2.md) and [b3](v4.6.0b3.md)
is in it, plus three fixes since b3. What you will notice after the update,
and whether you need to do anything (you don't):
[upgrade notes](../docs/UPGRADING.md#v460--from-v45x).

4.6.0 has three themes:

- **Prime robots get what Classic robots already had**, wherever the robot
  reports the data.
- **Missions become visible to automations and scripts**: when one
  starts, how it ended, which rooms it missed, what it used, and what a
  clean would take before it starts.
- **The documentation is arranged by task**, with a reference built from
  the code.

Everything new is additive. Existing entities, bus events and their fields
stay as they were.

---

## Prime robots: what Classic already had

The shared entities are now created on Prime too, **each once the robot
reports its field**:

- **Mission sensors**: start, elapsed time, id, recharge and expiry.
- **Lifetime figures**: average mission time, lifetime completion rate.
- **Dock counters and dock firmware.**
- **History sensors**: missions and completion rate over 30 days, last
  mission area, last error (code, time, room), the last mission summary,
  and the presence sensors.
- **Binary sensors**: mission active, mid-mission recharge, firmware
  updated, maintenance due, start blocked, demand clean blocked.
- **Other entities**: the stuck event, a floor plan of each stored map
  (disabled by default), the raw state, and room progress for missions
  started in the app or by a schedule.
- **A mission archive** of up to 800 missions, included in the backup.

What Prime still lacks and why: [V4/Prime robots](../docs/PRIME.md).

## A command the robot ignores

Sometimes a robot takes a start command without starting. **Every start
Roomba+ sends is now watched, on both generations**: the actions and the
start command, the automatic starts (blocking-sensor release,
`auto_clean_dirty_rooms`, demand cleaning), and the zone, favourite and
*Repeat last mission* buttons.

When the robot has not started 20 s (Classic) or 60 s (Prime) after the
command:

- **`roomba_plus_command_not_delivered`** fires, shown as *Command not
  delivered* on the new `event.<robot>_command`.
- **The command is sent once more**, but only when the robot is idle and
  reports no reason for refusing. The option *Send an ignored command once
  more* turns this off.
- **A Prime cloud document frozen on `run`** while the battery charges is
  reported as its own type, `state_frozen`.

**A Prime room clean whose sending the cloud did not confirm now fails the
action** (@Rickeeh, #248). It reported success before. The message says
what is known: the cloud did not confirm the command, so it may or may not
have reached the robot. Such a start is not sent a second time.

## Mission started, mission result, missed rooms

- **`roomba_plus_mission_started`** and `event.<robot>_mission_started`:
  who started the mission, `whole_home` or `rooms`, which rooms, their
  Home Assistant areas, and the mission number.
- **`roomba_plus_mission_result`** and `event.<robot>_mission_result`:
  `completed`, `completed_with_skips`, `cancelled`, `stuck_abandoned` or
  `error`, with the rooms that were skipped and the rooms asked for but
  not reached.
- **`roomba_plus.retry_missed_rooms`** cleans what the latest mission (or
  the one you name) skipped or did not reach.
- **`roomba_plus_room_completed`** carries the room's `area_id` and
  `area_name`.
- **`roomba_plus_start_blocked`** and **`_start_timeout`** name the robot
  (`entry_id`, `name`) and show on `event.<robot>_command`.

## Where the running mission is

`sensor.<robot>_current_room` and `sensor.<robot>_estimated_end_time`, on
both generations, beside mission progress. The room sensor lists each
room of the run as `pending`, `operating`, `skipped` or `completed`, and
names the room's Home Assistant area.

## Answers for scripts and Assist

Four response actions (`response_variable:` in a script). They read what
Roomba+ already holds and ask nothing of the cloud:

- **`get_rooms`**: the rooms and zones, with map, area, time estimate and
  how good that estimate is.
- **`get_missions`**: the newest stored missions, by `limit` and `days`.
- **`get_mission`**: one mission in full, with the battery it used and,
  per room, whether its time was learned and, if not, why.
- **`plan_clean`**: what a clean of the rooms given would take if started
  now, without starting it. With `end_by` the answer says whether it
  `fits`.

Each estimate carries its quality (runs, spread, `high` to `none`), and
each mission records what it was estimated to take.

## Battery, areas, and smaller additions

- **Battery per mission**: the last mission summary shows the battery at
  start and end, the use and the use per m². Its `battery_start_pct` and
  `battery_end_pct` existed since 3.1.0 and were always empty.
- **When each area was last vacuumed and mopped**:
  `sensor.<robot>_<area>_last_vacuumed` and `_last_mopped`, off by
  default, one pair per Home Assistant area the robot's rooms map to,
  across every Roomba+ robot in the household.
- **Send home after a pause** (Configure → Connection settings), off by default.
- **Debug capture**: `roomba_plus.debug_capture_start`, `_stop` and `_get`
  record a redacted debug log without editing `configuration.yaml`.
- **Blueprints arrive with the integration** under
  `blueprints/automation/roomba_plus/`. An existing file is never
  overwritten, and a blueprint you deleted does not come back.
- **Prime cloud streams**: four diagnostic sensors, off by default, show
  when each stream last delivered a message.

## Fixed

- **A plain charging dock is no Clean Base** (@azrael-229, #249). Any dock
  that reported a number for its state counted as a Clean Base, so a robot
  on a plain dock had its bag reported due every ~29 hours of runtime, and
  got the Clean Base sensors. Only a dock with its own firmware version or
  a state in the 300s counts now, the range the Clean Base status sensor
  reads. The Clean Base sensors created before stay as unavailable
  entities you can delete.
- **Layout spots from 4.5.3 are dropped once** (@azeiop, #246). The spots
  room cleans had collected before 4.5.4 stayed in the coverage history
  and kept `layout_change_detected` on. They are dropped on the first
  start; real changes are detected as before.
- **A short top-up is no battery trend** (@azeiop, #246). A charge counts
  toward *battery contact suspect* once it reached full or lasted two
  hours.
- **Counters survive a restart** (@tankdeer). *Total time*, the mission
  counts and the other lifetime counters keep their value until the robot
  reports again, instead of *Unknown*. The hour meter for the maintenance
  figures is kept from the robot's own message.
- **Device triggers that could never fire are no longer offered**, and
  `start_blocked` fires on Classic now.
- **Prime: the mission's initiator is a word again**, not the whole
  command with the robot's and map's ids.
- **Blueprints**: no file access on the event loop at start.

## In your language

- **Every action and its fields** is translated into the eight languages,
  including nine actions that showed in English everywhere, and the seven
  reset actions now have a description.
- **The choices in the action editor** are translated too: cleaning mode,
  pad wetness, weekdays, schedule frequency and the maintenance action.
  The editor writes the frequency in lower case now (`weekly`);
  automations with `WEEKLY` keep working. Pad wetness reads *Low*,
  *Medium* and *High*, as on the pad wetness select.
- **Three Prime parts have a name**: *Maintenance – Battery*,
  *Maintenance – Washing system (dock)* and *Maintenance – Washing system
  (mop)*. Their entity ids stay as they are.

## Removed

- The zone select and zone button for 900-series robots, which have not
  been created since 3.2.1, with their texts and icons.

## Under the hood

- `services.async_send_watched()` wraps every start; `command_watch` reads
  Classic's local state and Prime's `ro-currentstate.cleanMissionStatus`.
- New modules: `mission_started.py`, `mission_queries.py`,
  `estimate_quality.py`, `mission_battery.py`, `sensor_areas.py`,
  `pause_timeout.py`, `blueprint_install.py`, `debug_capture.py`,
  `prime_mission_archive.py`.
- `RoombaSensor` is a `RestoreSensor` for the lifetime counters.
- `services.yaml` holds fields and selectors only; every text is in
  `strings.json` and the translations.
- `scripts/build_reference.py` writes the reference pages;
  `scripts/check_doc_names.py` checks every entity id, action, event and
  menu path the docs name.

## Tested

- The full suite passes on Home Assistant 2025.5 and 2026.10; ruff, the
  typing baseline and the per-module coverage floors pass. Each fix's test
  fails without it.
- The generated entity reference matches all 124 Classic entities of a
  demo Home Assistant with a simulated Roomba 980.
- **Not yet confirmed on a robot**: the betas were out for two days, and
  no report from a robot running 4.6 has come back yet. The parts to watch
  first are Prime (the wait before *Command not delivered*, the frozen
  state, room progress from the timeline) and the 600 restart.
