# Roomba+ 4.6.8

**Classic robots that never acknowledge a subscription connect again,
keep-out zones show over requested positions, and the robot stands out on
the live map.**

Nothing to do: update and restart.

---

## Robots that never acknowledge a subscription (@gloopy-purple-liquid, #265; roombapy #624)

Some Classic robots never answer the subscription a client sends after
connecting: firmware 3.5.x on the Roomba 671, 675 and 694 by reports to
roombapy and Home Assistant, and, from the timing of its log, apparently
the Combo Essential. They send their state to a connected client anyway.
roombapy 2, which Roomba+ uses and Home Assistant's built-in integration
has used since 2026.11, waited for that answer, gave up after ten seconds and
dropped the connection, on every retry. These robots never connected.
The log shows `connection lost (Operation timed out), retrying in 1s`
about twelve seconds after connecting.

Roomba+ now waits three seconds for the answer and carries on without
it. A robot that answers does so in milliseconds and sees no change.

The fix belongs in roombapy and is proposed there (pschmitt/roombapy#624).
Until a release with it is installed, Roomba+ applies it to the roombapy
it loads, as it does for the closing of failed attempts from 4.6.7, and
steps aside once roombapy has it. Home Assistant's built-in integration,
if loaded, gets the change as well.

That the Combo Essential is one of these robots is not yet confirmed on a
robot.

## Keep-out zones and obstacles over requested positions (@jamd-pt)

On a robot whose live path comes from positions Roomba+ requests
(`position_source: request` on the map entity), the keep-out zones and
observed obstacles of the cloud map were never drawn. They now are, placed
like the floor plan under that path. @jamd-pt found it on an i7 with two
keep-out zones and worked out why: the overlay still assumed such a path
needs aligning, which the floor plan under it stopped needing in 4.4.0.

## The robot in magenta

The robot on the live map was drawn in the exact blue of its own path, so
on a map full of track a robot that had stopped was hard to find. It is
magenta now, a colour no other layer uses. Suggested by a user by mail.

## When the robot ignores a start and gives no reason (@nicosandller, #264)

The warning after a start the robot did not begin listed causes even when
the robot had given none, and was read as a diagnosis. With a reason, it
now points at `notReady` and `error`. Without one, it says so, names the
two cases where the robot ignores a command silently, and the check that
tells the robot's side from ours: whether the iRobot app can start it. If
it cannot either, the robot has stopped acting on commands, and pressing
CLEAN on it or restarting it clears that.

## Tested

- The full suite passes on Home Assistant 2025.5; the changed modules'
  tests pass on 2026.10. ruff, the typing baseline and the per-module
  coverage floors pass.
- The subscription fix against roombapy 2.0.2 and a stand-in robot that
  connects, publishes its state and never answers the subscription:
  without it, the connection fails after setup's sixteen seconds with the
  log line of #265; with it, the robot's state arrives after about three
  seconds.
- Each change's test fails without it.
- None of these changes has run on a robot yet.
