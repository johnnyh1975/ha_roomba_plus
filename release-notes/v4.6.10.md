## Record dock here (@jamd-pt)

The cleaning map places the dock where the robot charges after driving
home. That misses a dock that was moved, or a robot carried back to it.
@jamd-pt kept a local patch with a button for it, lost with every update.
It is part of Roomba+ now.

**Record dock here** (`button.<robot>_record_dock`, under *Configuration*
on the device) moves the dock to where the robot stands. The map draws it
anew and keeps it over a restart; during a mission, from the mission's
end.

It records only while the robot is on its dock (charging, or emptying its
bin into a Clean Base): elsewhere, the last position on the map is the
floor, not the dock. Pressed off the dock, or before the map has any
position, it says why and changes nothing. It stays available all the
time, so a dashboard card pointing at it shows no error.

Classic robots with a cleaning map.

If you used @jamd-pt's patch: the button keeps the patch's entity, as it
has the same unique ID; the patch can go.
