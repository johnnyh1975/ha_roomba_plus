# Roomba+ 4.6.4

**The room-tracking fixes of 4.6.3 now take effect, a Prime robot whose
cloud refuses a named shadow sets up, and the vacuum no longer carries an
error left from an ended mission.**

Needs **roombapy-prime 0.9.2**, as 4.6.3. Nothing to do after the update;
what you may notice: [upgrade notes](../docs/UPGRADING.md#v464--from-v463).

---

## Room tracking: the drive off the dock and the drive home (@willfanguy, #257)

4.6.3 meant to stop both from moving the current room. @willfanguy tested
it on his i4 against the cloud's timeline: being sent home now ended the
mission as it should, but the room still moved on the drive off the dock
and again as the robot docked.

- **The drive off the dock.** The message that ends a drive reports the
  robot cleaning and not driving, so it marked the room as worked in just
  before the check read that mark. Every drive that ended in a cleaning
  phase counted as leaving a room the robot had worked in, and 4.6.3's
  rule for the first minute had no effect. The mark now comes from the
  messages after the arrival. This was a mistake in 4.6.3; its test
  checked the rule, not what the check saw on arrival.
- **The drive home.** The room moved as the robot docked through the
  second route, the robot's phase changing to charging. While the robot
  heads for the dock, sent home or to recharge, neither route moves the
  room until it cleans again.

A drive inside a room still looks like one into the next, so on Smart Map
robots without positions the display can still run ahead.

## Prime: a named shadow the cloud refuses no longer fails setup (@takacstiborarp, #262)

A Roomba 205 (SKU L121240) did not set up: the iRobot cloud refuses the
subscription to its `rw-schedule` named shadow, and that one refusal
failed the whole entry, on every reload. Two other Prime robots on the
same account were fine. A refused shadow is now logged as a warning like
any other that cannot be read, and setup continues; what reads that
shadow, the schedule, stays empty.

## The vacuum carries no error left from an ended mission (#261)

The robot keeps its last error after it docks from a failed mission. The
error sensor has hidden such an error while the robot rests (no mission,
charging, stopped or idle); the vacuum's `error` and `error_code`
attributes still carried it. Both now follow the same rule. Pointed out
by @charchess.

The vacuum's state is unchanged: a robot that reports itself stuck is
shown as an error, on the dock or not.

## Tested

- The full suite passes on Home Assistant 2025.5 and 2026.10 with
  roombapy-prime 0.9.2; ruff, the typing baseline and the per-module
  coverage floors pass.
- Every fix's tests fail without it. The room-tracking tests now follow a
  drive from the dock to its arrival, which is what 4.6.3's did not.
- The room-tracking changes were tested against @willfanguy's drives and
  timeline; the shadow change against the error @takacstiborarp posted.
