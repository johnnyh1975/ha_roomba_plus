# Roomba+ 4.6.1

**Rooms get the area you mapped them to, and on Prime the selected
cleaning mode reaches every room.**

Built on **roombapy-prime 0.9.1**, unchanged. Nothing to do after the
update; what you will notice: [upgrade notes](../docs/UPGRADING.md#v461--from-v460).

---

## Rooms take their area from your mapping (@willfanguy, #253)

4.6.0 describes Home Assistant's mapping (*Map vacuum segments to
areas*) as what gives each room its area, in `get_rooms`, the
current-room sensor, the room events, the device tracker and the
*last vacuumed* and *last mopped* sensors. It never read it, on either
generation:

- it looked for keys Home Assistant does not use; Home Assistant
  stores the mapping as `area_mapping`, area first, with a list of
  segments;
- it built segment ids in forms Roomba+ does not offer (`<map>_rid_<id>`
  on Classic, `rid_<id>` on Prime).

So every room fell back to matching its name against the area names,
and a room named differently from its area got none. Roomba+ now
reads the mapping as Home Assistant stores it and looks up the ids it
offers (`<map>_<id>` on Classic, `rid_<map>/<id>` on Prime). A room on
another map is found by the name of its segment as Home Assistant
saved it. The area or alias of the same name stays the last resort.

## Prime: the cleaning mode on the select reaches every room (@AR2000AR, #254)

A room clean that names no mode, such as every start from the card,
takes the cleaning mode on the select. On Prime it reached the first
room only, and the other rooms ran in the robot's own mode: two rooms
picked in mop mode, the second was vacuumed. The mode now goes to
every room.

## Tested

- The full suite passes on Home Assistant 2025.5 and 2026.10; ruff, the
  typing baseline and the per-module coverage floors pass.
- The tests of both fixes fail on 4.6.0. The mapping tests are built
  from Home Assistant's own `area_mapping` shape and the segment ids
  Roomba+ offers.
