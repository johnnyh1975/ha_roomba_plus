# Roomba+ 4.6.3

**Room tracking stops counting the drives off the dock and home, a robot
sent home ends its mission, and Prime runs that got stuck or stopped
short get a result.**

Needs **roombapy-prime 0.9.2**, as 4.6.2. Nothing to do after the update;
what you may notice: [upgrade notes](../docs/UPGRADING.md#v463--from-v462).

---

## Room tracking on robots without positions (@willfanguy, #257)

A Smart Map robot that reports no position is followed by its drives: a
drive that ends after the robot cleaned in a room moves the display to the
next planned room. @willfanguy lined the drives of one run on his i4 up
against the cloud's timeline: five drives, two of them room changes. Two
of the other three are fixed:

- **The drive off the dock** moves no room. The i4 backs off the dock
  while reporting a cleaning phase, so the first room counted as worked in
  before the robot reached it, and a 61 s drive into the kitchen moved the
  display to the next room. A drive that starts in the mission's first
  minute now goes to the first room. A dock inside the first room is not
  affected: the robot cleans there first.
- **The drive home** moves no room. A drive that ends on the way to the
  dock (sent home, to recharge, after the mission) moved the display one
  room on as the robot left.

A drive inside a room still looks like one into the next, so the display
can still run ahead on such robots. With the iRobot account, the mission
record takes its rooms from the cloud's room events afterwards.

## A robot sent home ends its mission (@willfanguy, #257)

A mission with rooms of its plan not yet reached waits up to 90 seconds
before it counts a stop on the dock as its end, since some robots dock
between rooms. A robot the user sent home waited too. @willfanguy's
automation sent his i4 home and started it again two minutes later; both
runs were recorded as one mission, and neither fired
`roomba_plus_mission_completed` on time. A robot sent home is now
recorded as soon as it docks. A run resumed from there keeps the wait.

## Prime: `stuck` and `inc` runs get a result (@jdapaetz2, #260)

The mission history of @jdapaetz2's 405 Combo ended 14 of 32 runs with
`stuck` and 6 with `inc`, two codes Roomba+ did not know. All 20 were
filed as `unknown`, which no sensor counts: after a three-room run that
ended `inc`, *Area cleaned today*, the clean streak and the last mission
result stayed empty.

- `stuck` is filed as stuck, `inc` (a run that stopped short of its plan)
  as cancelled, the way the Classic cloud history reads the same field.
- Runs already stored as `unknown` take the result at the next sync of
  the history, so the history recovers without new runs.

## *Area cleaned today* counts every run

The sensor added up completed runs only, so a run that got stuck or was
stopped added nothing, though it cleaned what it covered. It now adds the
area of every run of the day that reports one, on Classic and Prime. The
clean streak and the completion rate still count completed runs only.

## Smaller changes

- **Setup names the built-in integration when it holds the robot.** A
  Classic robot takes one local connection at a time, and from Home
  Assistant 2026.11 the built-in Roomba integration loads beside Roomba+.
  When an active built-in entry has the robot, setup no longer says
  "Failed to connect", but that the built-in integration is connected and
  how to disable it. The troubleshooting entry for *Failed to connect*
  names it first.
- **Room select: rooms and zones told apart (#256).** The Classic room
  select says per entry whether it is a room or a zone (`segment_type`,
  as on Prime) and, where the floor plan has the zones' outlines, which
  room each zone lies in (`zone_room`), for a zone picker in the card.
- **Two fixes from @cburrell16 (#258).** A disabled Roomba+ entry no
  longer counts as a second connection to the robot in the *same robot*
  repair. The warning when live position gives up names the stalls
  counted and the last stall's cause.
