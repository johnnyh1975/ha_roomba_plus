# Roomba+ v4.0.0a32

Requires roombapy-prime **v0.3.0b3**.

Seven fixes, all from field reports, and four of them are the same shape: **code that
nothing reached.**

## The schedule that walked forward a day

@DaRealGuGu edited a Mon/Tue/Wed series and got Tue/Wed/Thu. His diagnostics carried the
proof — `days: [2, 3, 4]` — so the robot counts from **Sunday** and the table used when
writing a schedule started at Monday.

Not a shifted series and not a postponed Monday: **every day was written one further
along**, and the result still looked like a plausible three-day series. @utkjmitch noted
it compounds — edit weekly and the schedule orbits the calendar.

This module had **two** weekday tables disagreeing by one, and the second was only reached
once schedule *editing* existed. The write table is derived from the reading one now, so
they cannot drift again.

## Four things nothing was calling

@utkjmitch found `hass_ref` — a field read in two places and assigned in none. The Prime
profile store was never persisted and mission records re-imported from the cloud on every
restart. His note on why the tests missed it is the useful part: they assert on
`entry.runtime_data.hass_ref` **against a MagicMock, which invents attributes on demand.**

That shape turned out to be a search. Private functions whose name appears exactly once in
the component are definitions nothing reaches, and there were three more:

- **`_async_fetch_prime_time_estimates`** — the calendar reads its output to give schedule
  occurrences a real duration, and `mission_progress` needs it for a denominator. Both got
  `None` for the life of the feature. This is the other half of the trail he was following.
- **`_dock_position`** — the "seen beats remembered" correction written after his treadmill
  report. He called it unreachable in a frozen-shadow state; it was unreachable in every
  state.
- **`_readable_part_name`** — written after his a30 maintenance-list report and never
  connected to the line that builds the summary. His a31 observation that **the units
  changed and the names did not** was the proof.

The check is now a test, so the next one fails in CI rather than on a robot.

## Favourite buttons

@chairstacker's two favourites appear on v3.5.1 and not on the alpha. The server sometimes
wraps the list in an object; the Classic path has always unwrapped it and the Prime path
never did. Fixed in the library, and diagnostics now report the favourite count and
whether the buttons are enabled — his report had no way to tell those two apart.

## A false positive on its first morning

The `stale` phase detector shipped in a31 and @utkjmitch caught it misfiring the same day:
a recharge-and-resume enters `run` while the battery is still climbing from the charge —
genuinely running **and** genuinely rising, the one case where the mutual-exclusion test
lies.

Ten minutes of grace now, restarted on every transition into `run`. The freeze it exists
for rises for 61 hours, so waiting costs detection nothing. His patch, his tests.

## Also

- **Mission records had no error code** on a robot with 16 `stuck` endings, while the
  timelines carried 111 error events, 93 of them error 48. The record falls back to the
  first timeline error when the mission level says nothing.
- **Calendar summaries said `Zone 13`** for rooms named on another map. The a31 fix only
  filled names from the map being drawn; every map on the account is consulted now.
- **The maintenance list hides parts the robot denies having** — a dockless robot was
  being told about a dust bag. Explicit denial only: silence is not a denial.
- **Error codes carry iRobot's own text** as `error_title` and `error_description`
  attributes. Error 48 reads "An obstacle blocked the entrance to a room", which explained
  every incomplete mission in one tester's archive.
- **`has_stats`** answered "has the Classic path run" while appearing to answer "are there
  stats". It cost an hour of chasing the wrong absence.
- **Dirt cause classification** now works on Classic: rising dirt with declining speed is
  debris the brush is not picking up; rising dirt with steady speed is a dirty floor. The
  logic had been in the code since v3.2 and could never be called, because the trend it
  needs did not exist.

5,293 tests.
