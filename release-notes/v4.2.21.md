# Roomba+ 4.2.21

Per-room history on Prime robots, which never had a value, and each room
dated by when the robot finished it rather than by the end of the whole
mission.

## Prime: the per-room values were always empty

Everything Roomba+ knows per room on a Prime robot comes from the mission
timeline in the iRobot history:
- *Last cleaned – room* (option *Separate sensor per room and zone*),
- *Room cleaning history* and *Rooms overdue*, and with them
  `clean_overdue_rooms`,
- the rooms of each mission in the REST API,
- the measured room times behind mission progress.

The timeline gives its times as whole seconds, and the conversion
expected a different type. It failed on every room, silently, so none of
these ever had a value on a Prime robot. The tests built their timelines
in a shape the library never delivers, and passed throughout. @mrsnyds
ended up building his own room sensors because of it, and @chairstacker's
"every one of these reads Unknown" (#84) had this cause too, besides the
two fixed then.

- **Fixed.** Missions already stored are filled in from the iRobot
  history at the next sync, as far back as the cloud still lists them.
  The diagnostics download says how many records carry rooms
  (`mission_store.records_with_room_data` / `records_without_room_data`).
- **A room counts as cleaned by the same rule as on Classic:** a finished
  pass, or cleaned floor in a room closed when the mission ended — not a
  room the robot only reached into. So the room you send it home from
  counts once a tenth of its floor is done, as the iRobot app shows it;
  `last_duration_min` (below) tells a short stay apart. Saved clean
  zones count like rooms; no real Prime zone record has been checked
  for this yet.
- **The measured room times behind mission progress** learn only from
  cleaned rooms of at least half a minute, as on Classic. A room closed
  off now and then does not teach a few seconds.

## Each room is dated by when the robot finished it

*Last cleaned – room* and *Room cleaning history* gave every room of a
mission the time the whole mission ended. They now use the time the
robot was done with that room, taken from the timeline, on both
generations. A room without a time of its own keeps the mission's end.
*Rooms overdue* follows.

## Classic: *Last cleaned – room* showed the oldest clean

The per-room sensors read the mission history in the wrong direction and
showed the **oldest** clean of each room still in it, not the newest. Its
test listed the records newest first, the opposite of how the store
keeps them. Fixed, and tested through the store itself.

## How long a room took

- **`last_duration_min`** on *Last cleaned – room*: the time spent in that
  room on its newest clean, every visit added up.
- **`duration_sec`** on `roomba_plus_room_completed` and
  `event.*_room_completed`.
  - Classic: what room tracking measured.
  - Prime: from the room's own start in the timeline to the start of
    whatever came next — the drive into the room does not count.
  - `null` when the timeline gave no start for the room, for instance
    when Home Assistant started while the robot was in it.
