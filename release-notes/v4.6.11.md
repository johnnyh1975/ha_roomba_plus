# Roomba+ 4.6.11

**Three or more Prime robots on one account get their status again.**

Needs **roombapy-prime 0.9.5**; Home Assistant installs it with the
update. Nothing else to do: update and restart.

---

## Status on accounts with three or more Prime robots (@levidus)

Since 4.5.4, every robot of an iRobot account shares one cloud
connection. Each read of a robot's state kept two subscriptions on that
connection until it ended, and the iRobot cloud allows 50 per
connection. @levidus's three Prime robots needed 54: the cloud refused
the rest, the status updates among them, and all three entries failed
their status refresh while looking docked.

roombapy-prime 0.9.5 keeps those subscriptions for the eight most
recently read parts of the state and releases older ones. @levidus
found the cause, reproduced it, and confirmed it with a local patch on
his three robots; the released fix takes a bounded approach of its own,
not yet run on a robot.

This may also be what turned down the status of three Combo Essentials
on one account over the cloud (#265): the same error, on an account
with three robots. If you saw the repair issue *Classic robot: the
cloud sends no status*, it may go away after this update.
