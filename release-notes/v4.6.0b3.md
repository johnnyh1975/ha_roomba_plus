# Roomba+ 4.6.0b3

**The third beta of 4.6.0.** It needs *Include pre-releases* for Roomba+ in
the Marketplace (*Show beta versions* in HACS before Home Assistant 2026.11)
and is built on **roombapy-prime 0.9.1**, unchanged. Everything in
[4.6.0b1](v4.6.0b1.md) and [4.6.0b2](v4.6.0b2.md) is in it. This beta
rebuilds the documentation around tasks, and fixes what that review found
in the integration: actions without a translation or description, three
Prime sensors without a name, and texts of entities that no longer exist.
Robot behaviour is unchanged from b2.

Please report back:

- **Prime robots**: the battery, dock washing system and mop washing system
  sensors should show a name (*Maintenance – Battery*, *Maintenance –
  Washing system (dock)*, *Maintenance – Washing system (mop)*) instead of
  the robot's name alone.
- **Home Assistant in a language other than English**: every Roomba+ action
  and its fields should appear in your language in the action editor.
- **The documentation**: a page that does not answer what you came for, or
  says something your robot does differently.
- Everything b1 and b2 asked for still stands.

  installing to the first room clean, with screenshots.

  every page.

  Getting started, and screenshots that show on every branch and release.

## Actions in your language

- Nine actions had no translation and showed in English in every language:
  `reset_robot_profile`, `advance_room`, `explain_mission`,
  `clean_overdue_rooms`, `auto_clean_dirty_rooms`, `run_favorite` and the
  three Prime schedule actions (`create_schedule`,
  `update_schedule`, `delete_schedule`). Five more had fields without one. All are
  translated into the eight languages now, and ten names still in English
  in Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese are translated too.
- The seven reset actions (`reset_filter`, `reset_brush`, `reset_battery`,
  `reset_pad`, `reset_wheel_cleaning`, `reset_contact_cleaning`,
  `reset_bin_cleaning`) showed with no description. They are described now
  and take a Roomba+ vacuum as target.
- `clean_room` says where its room names come from: the names the room
  select shows, from the iRobot account or given under Configure.

**Correction to the b2 notes:** they said `roomba_plus.reset_battery` does
not exist and is a button only. It exists as an action as well, as do the
other six reset actions; the buttons and the actions both work.

## Three Prime parts get a name

The battery, the dock's washing system and the mop washing system had no
name, so their sensors showed the robot's name only. They are
*Maintenance – Battery*, *Maintenance – Washing system (dock)* and
*Maintenance – Washing system (mop)* now, in all eight languages. The
entity ids stay as they are.

## Removed

- The zone select and zone button for 900-series robots. Neither has been
  created since 3.2.1; their code, texts and icons are gone. The
  documentation no longer promises a 900-series area select.
- Texts and icons of the old favourite button and the cleaning speed trend
  sensor, which no longer exist.

## Under the hood

- `services.yaml` carries fields and selectors only; names and descriptions
  come from `strings.json` alone, so the two can no longer disagree.
- `scripts/build_reference.py` writes the entity, action and option pages
  from the code, `strings.json` and `services.yaml`; `--check` fails when
  they are out of date, and a test runs it.
- `scripts/check_doc_names.py` checks every entity id, action, event and
  menu path named anywhere under `docs/` against the code.
- `scripts/check_service_schemas.py` reads action names built in a loop, so
  it covers the reset actions.
- `scripts/check_markdown_links.py` checks every page under `docs/`,
  including anchors set with `<a id>`.
- `AGENTS.md`: documentation describes how things are now, each section
  starts with an *Applies to* line, and the reference pages are generated.

## Tested

- The full suite passes on Home Assistant 2025.5 and 2026.10 in CI; ruff,
  the typing baseline and the per-module coverage floors pass.
- The 13 new tests (every action in `services.yaml`, every action and field
  named and described in every language, no text in `services.yaml`, every
  Prime part named, every entity name belongs to an entity the code
  creates, loop-built action names read) fail against b2.
- The generated entity reference matches all 124 Classic entities of a demo
  Home Assistant with a simulated Roomba 980. The Prime rows are read from
  the code and not yet compared with a real Prime robot.
