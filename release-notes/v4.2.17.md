# Roomba+ 4.2.17

## The maintenance to-do list can be switched on for Classic robots

Since 4.0 the maintenance to-do list (`todo.<robot>_maintenance`) is
opt-in: *Show maintenance as a to-do list* in the integration options,
off by default. The switch was only ever added to the options of Prime
robots. **A Classic robot's list could not be switched on at all.**

@ScenicSystemsLLC found it with three Classic robots on one account.
After removing one robot and adding it again, its list was gone, while
the other two still showed theirs. Theirs were not working either:
they were `unavailable` and `restored`, left behind in the entity
registry from before the list became opt-in.

- **Classic robots have the switch now**, under Configure → Connection
  settings → *Show maintenance as a to-do list*. Switch it on and the
  list comes back, under the same entity id as long as nothing else has
  taken it.
- **A list that is switched off leaves nothing behind.** The left-over
  `unavailable` to-do entities are removed on the next start, as
  switched-off calendars already were. A dashboard card that still
  points at one shows "Entity not found" until the list is switched on
  again.
