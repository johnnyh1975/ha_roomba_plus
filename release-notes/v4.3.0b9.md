# Roomba+ 4.3.0b9

**A beta.** Everything in [4.3.0b8](v4.3.0b8.md), plus the fixes from
the stable release [4.2.21](v4.2.21.md).

Needs *Show beta versions* in HACS. Built on **roombapy-prime 0.5.0b2**,
as b8 was.

---

## From 4.2.21: per-room history on Prime, rooms dated by their own end

- **Prime robots had no per-room values at all** — *Last cleaned – room*,
  *Room cleaning history*, *Rooms overdue*, the rooms in the REST API and
  the measured room times. The mission timeline's times are whole
  seconds and the conversion expected another type, so it failed on
  every room, silently. Fixed; missions already stored are filled in at
  the next history sync. Rooms count as cleaned by Classic's rule, and
  saved zones count like rooms (@mrsnyds, @chairstacker #84).
- **Each room is dated by when the robot finished it,** not by the end
  of the whole mission, on both generations.
- **Classic: *Last cleaned – room* showed the oldest clean** still in
  the history. It shows the newest now.
- **How long a room took:** `last_duration_min` on *Last cleaned – room*,
  `duration_sec` on `roomba_plus_room_completed`.

Details: [4.2.21](v4.2.21.md).
