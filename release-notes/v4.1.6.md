# Roomba+ 4.1.6

Three fixes, all found in field captures from testers.

## Cleaning a zone started a mission that ended immediately

Pressing **Clean selected room** with a zone selected sent the robot
out, and it came straight back — the mission recorded as completed after
about a minute with nothing cleaned.

The robot's map data lists every region, zones included, and marks each
one as a room or a zone. Roomba+ ignored that marking and offered every
region as a room. A zone's id then went out as a *room* id, and since no
room has that number the robot found nothing to clean and reported
success.

@theChef163's zone is region 100 on a robot whose rooms are 10–19. His
mission record carries the command that shows it, which is what made
this findable rather than guessable.

Zones are now recognised as zones and offered as such.

## Gentle mode was missing on S9-series robots

The switch appeared only when the robot reported the setting as
`gentle`. S9+ robots report the same feature as `gentleMode` and
advertise it in their capabilities, so the switch was never created for
them (@ScenicSystemsLLC). Both names are accepted now.

## An empty navigation record counted as a measurement

Two of three robots on the same firmware report their mission navigation
statistics with every counter at zero — no landmarks seen, none tracked
— during a mission and after it (@veronoicc). A robot navigating by
camera does not see zero landmarks; those records are simply never
filled in.

Roomba+ fed the relocalisation count from them into a per-robot measure
of localisation quality, so on those robots the measure was built from
numbers the robot never took. Empty records are ignored now; a genuine
zero still counts.
