# Roomba+ 4.6.12

**Repair issues say which robot they are about.**

Nothing to do: update and restart.

---

## Every repair about one robot names it (@lloydbayley)

With several robots, *Dock contact health issue detected* said nothing
about which robot it meant. Every repair issue about one robot now
starts its title with the robot's name, in all eight languages. When
the dock contact issue is raised, the log also gets one warning naming
the robot and its three counters.

Four issues had one id for every robot, so one robot's check could
clear or replace another robot's notice: the runtime counter reset,
the cloud's obstacle zones, the new-rooms notice and the layout-change
areas. Each robot has its own now. Removing a robot clears all of its
repair issues, the layout-change ones included, and leaves the other
robots' alone.

Also in the dock contact issue: it now says to wipe the contacts with a
slightly damp cloth and let them dry, as iRobot's care advice does; it
said a dry cloth, and five languages showed the text in English. The
new-rooms notice showed an empty dialog, its text filed under a step the
dialog never opens.

## The washable pad of a Classic Combo (@jamd-pt)

iRobot's cloud counts the washable pad of a Combo j9+ and similar
Classic robots in missions. Roomba+ read that count but showed no
maintenance entry for it: on a Classic robot it named a pad only on a
Braava. The pad is now a maintenance entry with iRobot's count, and
marking it done resets the count at iRobot, as the app does. The reset
over the cloud for a Classic robot has not been run on a robot yet.
