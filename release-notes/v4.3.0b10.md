# Roomba+ 4.3.0b10

**A beta.** Everything in [4.3.0b9](v4.3.0b9.md), plus the fixes from the
stable release [4.2.22](v4.2.22.md).

Needs *Show beta versions* in HACS. Built on **roombapy-prime 0.5.0b2**,
as b9 was.

---

## From 4.2.22: `clean_room` could clean a zone instead of the room

Rooms and zones are numbered separately, so a Classic map can hold room
`0` and zone `0` at once. The zone overwrote the room, and `clean_room`
sent the room's name out as the zone: on @FJSoninC's j7+, *Hallway*
cleaned a 9 sq ft toilet zone, twice. A zone's name now resolves to an
id that carries its type, as `clean_zone` and Prime already do.

## From 4.2.22: Prime per-room sensors, and names instead of numbers

On Prime, the per-room sensors (*Separate sensor per room and zone*)
were created only if the map was read before the sensors were set up,
and *Rooms – Cleaning history* showed region numbers after a restart
until the robot next sent something (@mrsnyds, Roomba 105). The
announcement that the room names had arrived was never sent. Fixed. The
same mistake also kept `clean_room` from using the *Cleaning mode*
selector as its default, and kept the Classic maintenance baseline from
being saved.

## From 4.2.22: the cleaning history as one attribute

*Rooms – Cleaning history* also carries its rooms under `rooms`, so a
template can list them without filtering out other attributes. The flat
attributes stay.

## From 4.2.22: typical time per room, names across restarts

- *Last cleaned – room* gains `typical_duration_min`: the median time
  the robot spent in the room over its newest 10 cleans, charging not
  counted (@mrsnyds).
- Prime room names are kept across restarts.
- The per-room sensors read the mission history once per update between
  them, not once each.
- Removing the integration now also removes the Prime live-map bundle.

Details: [4.2.22](v4.2.22.md).
