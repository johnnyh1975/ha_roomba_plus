# Roomba+ 4.6.5

**Favourites start after a map is retrained, and the cleaning path puts
the dock where the robot docks.**

Needs **roombapy-prime 0.9.2**, as 4.6.4. Nothing to do after the update;
what you may notice: [upgrade notes](../docs/UPGRADING.md#v465--from-v464).

---

## Favourites after a map retrain (@Jamd-PT, #263)

A favourite on a Classic robot carries the map version it was saved with.
After the map was retrained in the iRobot app, pressing a favourite button
or calling `roomba_plus.run_favorite` sent that old version: the robot
took the start, cleaned nothing and reported error 224, while the same
favourite worked from the app. Every room command has looked up the map's
current version since 4.2.13; these two did not.

They do now, the same way, and so does *Repeat last mission*, which had
its own copy of the lookup. Without a current version the saved one is
sent as before, with a warning in the log. The favourite itself is not
changed.

## The dock on the cleaning path (@Jamd-PT, #263)

A Smart Map robot that publishes no position of its own has its positions
asked for while it moves. Those come in the cloud map's frame, whose
origin is not the dock, but the dock was drawn at the origin all the
same: on the dock, the robot and the dock marker were apart.

When such a robot drives itself home and starts charging, the dock is now
drawn where its last position was. It is kept across missions and
restarts. A robot carried back to the dock after stopping somewhere does
not move it. Positions arrive only while the robot moves, so the last one
is a few seconds before docking and the marker is close rather than
exact. Until the first drive home after the update, the dock stays at the
origin.

## Tested

- The full suite passes on Home Assistant 2025.5 and 2026.10 with
  roombapy-prime 0.9.2; ruff, the typing baseline and the per-module
  coverage floors pass.
- The tests of both changes fail without them, except the one that checks
  that a carried robot does not move the dock.
- @Jamd-PT confirmed the favourite fix on his robot with his own patch,
  which this matches. The dock change differs from his patch and has not
  been on a robot yet.
