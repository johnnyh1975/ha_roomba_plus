# Roomba+ 4.6.2

**A zone clean is followed zone by zone, and a Prime robot whose
topic prefix changes rebuilds its connection.**

Needs **roombapy-prime 0.9.2** (4.6.1: 0.9.1); Home Assistant installs it
with the update. Nothing to do after the update; what you may notice:
[upgrade notes](../docs/UPGRADING.md#v462--from-v461).

---

## A zone clean is followed zone by zone (@cwar36, #252)

The current room follows the robot's position from room to room. A zone
clean lists its zones, not rooms, whether it was started from a button,
a script or the iRobot app, so no position ever matched it: the display
stayed on the first zone. Two zones in one room could not have been told
apart by their room in any case.

The cloud map draws every clean zone with its outline, as it draws the
keep-out zones; Roomba+ did not read those outlines. It does now: during
a zone clean, a position inside one of the run's zones counts for that
zone, and the display moves on from zone to zone, also within one room.
Only the zones of the running clean are looked at, so a zone the robot
only crosses does not count. Between zones, the robot is in its room as
before.

The diagnostics now count the map's clean zones (`umf_zone_shapes.zones`).
Whether a robot's map carries their outlines is shown there; @cwar36's
j9+ is the first to report. Without outlines, nothing changes.

## Prime: a changed topic prefix rebuilds the robot (@Rickeeh, #255)

A Prime robot sends and receives under a topic prefix that belongs to
its iRobot deployment. roombapy-prime 0.9.2 reports when a renewed login
gives a robot a different prefix than it was built with. Roomba+ then
reloads the entry, which builds the robot again with the new prefix:

- at most once per entry in four hours; a further change within that
  time is logged as a warning, and the robot keeps its prefix until the
  entry is reloaded or Home Assistant restarts;
- no command is sent again. A start being watched at that moment is
  neither reported as not delivered nor resent, since whether it arrived
  can no longer be told; the diagnostics record it as `prefix_changed`.

Such a move during a running session has not been seen on a robot yet.

## Tested

- The full suite passes on Home Assistant 2025.5 and 2026.10 with
  roombapy-prime 0.9.2; ruff, the typing baseline and the per-module
  coverage floors pass.
- The zone tests fail on 4.6.1 (8 of 9; the ninth checks that a room
  clean is unchanged). The tests of #255 fail without its change (11).
- Zone outlines were tested with a map built like the cloud's, not yet
  with @cwar36's.
