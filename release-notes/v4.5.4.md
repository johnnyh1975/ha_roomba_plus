# Roomba+ 4.5.4

**Two Prime robots on one account stop knocking each other off, the
rooms map follows the map you cleaned and keeps its rooms, Prime maps
show what was vacuumed and what was mopped, *Vacuum and mop* reaches
every room, room cleans no longer flood Repairs, and the maintenance
figures survive a restart and a part replaced early.**

Needs **roombapy-prime 0.9.1** (4.5.3: 0.8.3); Home Assistant installs it
with the update. Betas: [4.5.4b1](v4.5.4b1.md), [b2](v4.5.4b2.md); the Prime map colours,
the favourites log and the j8+ came after b2.

---

## The rooms map keeps its rooms after a new map version

When a Smart Map got a new version (an edit in the iRobot app, or a
version the cloud makes on its own), Roomba+ rebuilt the rooms map from
the room list. That list has the names but not the outlines, so the
rooms map stayed empty until Home Assistant restarted. It is now rebuilt
from the same data as at startup, which carries the outlines, and with
the door width set under Configure. Found in the code, not reported.

## More than one Smart Map: the floor plan shows the map you cleaned (@scenicsystemsllc)

With more than one Smart Map, the rooms map showed the map the cloud
listed first, while the room names came from the map edited most
recently, and neither had to be the map the robot had just cleaned.
@scenicsystemsllc keeps extra maps for areas his robots are carried
into, and a mission there never showed on the map.

Now:

- **One map**: that map, as before.
- **More than one**: the map the robot's last command went to. Clean a
  room of another map, and once the mission's cloud update is in, the
  rooms map shows that map.
- **No such command** (a start that named no map), or the robot answered
  *Smart Map localization failed*: the map edited most recently, the one
  the room list uses.

The map changes with the cloud update at the end of the mission, not
while it runs. To pick rooms of a map that is not the one edited most
recently, enable that map's zone select: it is created disabled and
named "(inactive)".

Only what is shown changes. Room and zone commands still go to the map
of their room, and the account's own "active map" in the iRobot cloud is
not touched. Rooms named in the naming form are saved for the map the
form showed. The diagnostics name the map shown and why
(`floor_plan_pmap_id`, `floor_plan_map_source`).

## A floor plan of each map (@molnarpeterhu)

Each Smart Map stored in the account gets its own picture,
`image.<robot>_floor_plan_<map>`, named after the map ("Floor plan
Upstairs"): its rooms with their names, walls and doors, the same
drawing as the rooms map, but always of that one map and without the
robot or the cleaning path.

They are **created disabled**, so nothing appears on a dashboard and
nothing is read from the cloud until you enable one. An enabled floor
plan is read once per map version; the map the rooms map shows costs no
extra call. Room names come from the map itself or from the naming
notice for that map, not from aliases, which are kept by room number
while room numbers repeat across maps. A map deleted in the app is
removed at the next start.

The rooms map's own picture is unchanged: it now draws through the same
function, and gives the same bytes.

## "Vacuum segments have changed" with nothing to remap (@adamvig)

On a Classic robot with zones, the repair "Vacuum segments have changed"
came back every few days, and the dialog it opened had nothing to remap.
The check compared your saved mapping with the active map's rooms alone,
while the list Home Assistant shows also has the zones, so the two never
matched. It now compares with the same list, for the maps your mapping
names: a mapping made on one floor does not count as changed because the
robot went to another.

A second fault in the same list: a zone whose number no room has (his
"Front Door", zone 0) was offered twice, as a zone and as a room. Mapped
to an area, the room entry would have been sent as room 0. Zones are now
offered once, as zones.

You will see the repair once more after updating if your mapping holds
such a duplicate: open it and save, and it stays away.

## Prime maps show what was vacuumed and what was mopped (@tar-gezed, #245)

When the robot labels its paths and coverage with the cleaning mode, the
**Room map** and **Live cleaning map** of a Prime robot now draw them
apart: vacuumed in green, mopped in blue, both at once in teal. Paths the
robot only drove along are grey and do not count as cleaned. A robot
whose map data carries no modes keeps the colours it had.

During a run, the robot's live positions still draw the path. Once the
run has ended, the robot's own record of the paths replaces them, but
only if it arrived during that run; a record from an earlier mission,
kept over a restart, never does. A short run that sends no new record
keeps its live path. The coverage also follows the map shown, as the
robot's position already did.

Seen on a Roomba Plus 416 Combo. Contributed by @tar-gezed.

## Two Prime robots on one account stop knocking each other off (@Baby-scarf, @mattdmrc)

One iRobot login gives every robot of an account one connection token,
with one client id, and the iRobot cloud allows one connection per
client id: when a second one connects, it drops the older one. Roomba+
opened a connection per Prime robot, so two Prime robots on one account
took turns dropping each other, every couple of seconds, for as long as
both ran. @Baby-scarf's log counted about 120 drops in five minutes on
each robot, and the values lagged by minutes to hours.

roombapy-prime 0.9.0 gives the Prime robots of one token **one shared
connection**. A drop is rebuilt once for all of them, the token is
renewed once, and unloading one robot leaves the others connected.
@Baby-scarf checked the shared connection on his account first: two
robots, 75 minutes, a token refresh, no drop.

Nothing to do. One Prime robot, or Classic robots, see no difference.

## "Vacuum and mop" mopped the first room only (@tar-gezed)

On a Prime robot with *Vacuum and mop* selected, Home Assistant's own
start sends every room of the map with the mode. The mode went with the
first room only, and the robot vacuumed the rest. @tar-gezed found it on
a Roomba Plus 416 Combo and traced it to the call: the per-room settings
are a list by position, and a list of one reached one room. Every room
now gets the mode.

The zone action had the same fault for two zones or more, with the mode,
smart scrub, pad wetness and two passes. Every zone gets them now. The
room action `roomba_plus.clean_room` was right all along.

## Layout-change repairs by the hundred after room cleans

Three single-room cleans in a row on a tester's i7 created 598 "Possible
layout change detected" repairs at once, one per 15 cm spot of the rest
of the floor. Nothing had moved.

The layout-change check remembers, for each spot, whether the last
missions covered it, and flags a spot that was covered reliably and then
missed three times. A mission sent to one room counted as having missed
every spot of every other room; three of them flagged the whole house.

- **Only missions sent everywhere count now.** A mission sent to rooms or
  zones still feeds the coverage map, but not the layout-change history.
  Both the live path and the cloud path (robots like the i7, whose
  firmware sends no positions) go by what the mission was sent to.
- **One repair per area**, not per spot: neighbouring spots make one
  issue, placed at the area's centre. A dismissal covers the spots it was
  shown for; an area that grows is shown again.
- **Repairs whose spots are covered again go.** The code said so and only
  cleared its dismissal records; the issues stayed until a restart.

## Parts read as new after a restart (@tankdeer)

A Roomba 600 does not send its hour meter again when Home Assistant
reconnects to it; it sends it with its next mission. Until then Roomba+
counted with 0 hours, so every part read as new: @tankdeer's filter ran
down to 59 h and showed 60 h for a day after a restart, until the next
run. A reset pressed in that window was recorded at hour 0, which made
the new part as old as the robot once the meter came back.

Roomba+ now keeps the last reading it saw, saved at most once per
runtime hour, and counts with it until the robot sends a new one. A
lower reading from the robot is still used as it is: that is a firmware
resetting the meter, and the repair says so. The first reading is kept
from the first message that carries one after the update, so on a 600
the fix takes effect after its next mission. Diagnostics show it as
`maintenance.last_known_hr`.

## A new filter read 2 hours left (@rodelero1)

Roomba+ learns a part's life from the hours between its replacements.
On an i7, two filter resets an hour of runtime apart taught it a filter
life of one hour. The filter fitted next read due after four hours; he
replaced it and pressed the reset, and the median of one and four hours
gave the new filter 2 hours left, due again soon after. Each early
replacement taught the next.

A gap shorter than a quarter of the part's series interval (13 h for a
52 h filter) is no longer learned: a part swapped that early was not
worn out. The check is made when the history is read, so a history
already stored is corrected by the update, without anything to do. Gaps
of a normal length are learned as before.

## No more favourites warning every 15 minutes (@Baby-scarf)

On an account with two Prime robots, each one logged "1 of 1
favourite(s) were not offered as buttons" every fifteen minutes: the
favourite list is the account's, each robot shows only its own, and the
other robot's favourite was counted as dropped. Only a favourite with no
usable id is reported now, once until the count changes. Deleted,
hidden, internal and other robots' favourites are logged at debug.

## A Roomba j8+ added from the account is Classic (roombapy-prime 0.9.1)

A j8+ (`j855020`) added from the iRobot account was set up cloud-only:
its SKU was not in the table that tells the two robot generations apart,
and an unknown robot takes the Prime path. Its firmware is the j7's
platform; the j8+ is Classic and now gets the local connection on a new
setup.

An existing entry keeps working over the cloud. To move it to the local
connection, *Reconfigure* it with the robot's IP address and leave the
password empty: Roomba+ fetches it from the account.

The warning about an unknown SKU said "Everything should work". For a
Classic robot it did not: it had lost its local connection. It now says
the robot works over the cloud, and how a Classic robot gets its local
connection back.

## Mission history on a Roomba Plus 416 (@tar-gezed, #238)

The history of a Roomba Plus 416 carries no `missionId`, and Roomba+
dropped every record without one: "imported 0 missions" while the app
showed 22. A record without it now gets an id from its mission number
and start time (`p_nmssn_7_1786000000`), the same on every read, so a
repeated sync adds nothing twice. Records with a `missionId` keep their
id. Contributed by @tar-gezed.

## Under the hood

- roombapy-prime 0.9.1 (was 0.8.3): one MQTT connection per connection
  token (`RobotChannel`, `ConnectionShare`, 0.9.0) and the j8+ as Classic
  (0.9.1). The manifest and the CI workflows install it.
- `umf_aligner.aligner_from_umf()` builds the aligner for setup, rebuild
  and bootstrap; `UmfAligner.pmap_id`.
- `cloud_coordinator.displayed_pmap()` and `newest_pmap_id()`;
  `active_pmap_id` is unchanged. A change of map rebuilds the aligner
  even when the two versions are equal. `const.door_width_mm()`.
- `room_cleaning.classic_room_segments()`, `classic_zone_segments()`,
  `classic_segment_ids()`: one source for the segment list and its check.
- `image.draw_room_layout_png()` (shared by the rooms map and the floor
  plans), `RoombaFloorPlanImage`; `cloud_coordinator.async_floor_plan()`,
  `stored_maps()`; `umf_aligner.umf_room_layout()`.
- `vacuum._prime_start_whole_home()` and the zone action write each run
  setting once per room or zone.
- `GridStore.update_from_mission(whole_home=...)`, decided by
  `image._mission_was_whole_home()` and the record's `scope`;
  `repairs.furniture_areas()`.
- `MaintenanceStore.hours()`, `note_hours()`, `last_known_hr` (stored);
  `_learned_hours()` drops gaps under `LEARNED_MIN_SHARE` (0.25) of the
  series interval.
- Prime maps: `image._prime_map_mode()`, the mode colours,
  `PrimeRoomsImage._closed_with_fresh_bundle()` and
  `RoombaData.prime_trail_started_at` (#245).
- `prime_favorites.async_favorites_attribute()` sorts what it drops by
  reason and warns once.

## Tested

- The full suite passes on Home Assistant 2025.5, 2026.10 and 2026.11.0;
  ruff, the typing baseline and the per-module coverage floors pass.
- Each new test fails without its change.
- **Confirmed on robots**: two Prime robots sharing one connection
  (@Baby-scarf, five hours on b2 without a drop, after a 75-minute
  check of the connection with the library's tool). The cause of the
  600's lost maintenance figures was confirmed by @tankdeer; the fix
  itself was not yet.
- **Not yet confirmed on a robot**: the rooms map after a new map

  the segment repair staying away with zones; *Vacuum and mop* on every
  room and the zone settings; the layout-change history; both
  maintenance fixes; the mode colours in a live run (drawn from a
  recorded mission of a Plus 416 Combo, replayed); the favourites
  warning; the j8+ on its local connection.
