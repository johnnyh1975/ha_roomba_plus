# Roomba+ v4.0.0a12

Fixes for three things reported within hours of a11 — two of them mine.

## Room cleaning now actually reachable from the UI

a11 advertised the capability without implementing Home Assistant's own side of it. The "Map vacuum
segments to areas" dialog opened and was empty, because the method that supplies the room list
returned nothing for Prime robots. The feature looked present and did nothing, which is worse than
not offering it — the user spends their time looking for their own mistake.

Rooms now appear in that dialog, and the Clean Area button works from a standard tile card.

## Consumable sensors were showing minutes as if they were hours

The API reports minutes for the time-based parts; the app displays hours. So a filter with 33 hours
left showed as `1980`, unitless. Two testers spotted it by comparing against their own app.

Also fixed:

- **Real part names**, in eight languages. English and French come from the app itself — both
  testers matched their part numbers independently and agreed. The rest are translations.
- **Naming now matches Classic.** These were "Consumable – …" while every Classic consumable is
  "Maintenance – …", which read like two integrations in one entity list.
- **Units come from the server**, not a hardcoded table. A diagnostics download showed the API
  states `count_type` per part; inferring it from screenshots was right and still the wrong place
  to put it.

Parts 202 and 212 keep their numbers. Both report `pad_washes_used` and differ only by category,
and neither tester could find either one in the app. A made-up label gets believed; a bare number
invites a question.

## The map survives a restart

Prime displays a PNG that iRobot renders, and it only arrives during and after a mission — so any
restart or update left the entity blank until the robot next ran. The last map is now stored and
restored.

The a11 change to report "unavailable" rather than a white square did not cause this. It made it
visible: both states mean "no map", but one of them looks like a broken image.

**Groundwork for something better:** the same stream also carries position samples — the exact
input the Classic map renderer draws from, which is why Classic maps have room labels and keep-out
zones and this one does not. Diagnostics now count those samples, because whether rendering our own
map is worthwhile depends entirely on how many actually arrive.
