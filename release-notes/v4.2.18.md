# Roomba+ 4.2.18

## Switching the maintenance list on no longer breaks the reload

4.2.17 gave Classic robots the switch for the maintenance to-do list.
Switching it on and saving then failed. The integration page showed
"failed to unload", the log showed `ValueError: Config entry was never
loaded!`, and the list only appeared after a full restart of Home
Assistant (@ScenicSystemsLLC, on all three of his robots).

**Why.** Saving the options reloads the robot's entry. To unload it,
Roomba+ rebuilt the list of its platforms from the options, which now
included the to-do list. The to-do platform had never been loaded, and
Home Assistant refused the entire unload because of it. Switching the
calendar back on after having it off would have failed the same way.

- **The unload now takes down exactly what the setup loaded**, whatever
  the options say by then.
- **Switching the maintenance list on or off reloads the entry
  deliberately.** Before, the reload happened only by accident, because
  another field on the same form happened to differ. Saving the form
  without changing anything no longer reloads.

If you hit this with 4.2.17, a restart has already set things right.
Nothing else is needed.

## The rooms map

On Classic robots:
- **"Draw room names" now takes effect when you save it.** The map image
  was kept until the map itself changed, so switching names on, or
  naming a zone, showed nothing new before that. The Prime rooms map is
  redrawn on saving too.
- **The names are the ones you use everywhere else.** In order: your own
  alias, the name from your iRobot account, then a name typed into the
  naming notice.
- **A room without a name shows its number**, as on the Prime map: the
  naming notice lists zones by number.

Names on the map are off by default, because the xiaomi-vacuum-map-card
draws its own. Switch them on under Configure → Connection settings →
*Draw room names on the map image*. The feature guide had this listed as
on by default (@liblit).
