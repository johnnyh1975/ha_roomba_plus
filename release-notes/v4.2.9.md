# Roomba+ 4.2.9

## The countdown hit zero while rooms were still to come

The remaining-time figure worked out which room you were in by adding
up the per-room estimates until they passed the elapsed time. Once the
mission ran longer than the whole estimate, no room matched and the
countdown fell to zero — with three of four rooms done and the fourth
not started.

It now uses the room your robot is actually in, and counts the rest of
that room plus the rooms after it.

This surfaced *because* 4.2.8 works: measured room times replaced a
generous average with real, shorter figures, so the mission outran its
own estimate (@Thonno).

## Diagnostics explain an unaligned map

`aligner_aligned: false` looked like a fault and usually is not one.
The alignment needs door positions from your floor plan *and* markers
from observed positions; a robot that never publishes its position has
the first and never the second. Room tracking works regardless — only
the drawn room outlines are affected.

Downloads now say that, and count the missions carrying the events the
fallback would need. One tester removed and re-added his device chasing
this, which the field gave him no way to avoid.

## "Two Roomba integrations installed" for people who have one

The notice fires when Home Assistant's built-in Roomba integration is
set up alongside this one — they need different versions of the same
library and one will fail to load. That part is right.

It was also counting entries that never load: a discovered robot you
clicked "Ignore" on, or a disabled entry. Neither installs the library,
so neither can clash — and neither shows in your integrations list, so
the notice pointed at something you could not find or remove.

Reported by two users, one after a full restart and shutdown
(@boelle, @mermr1).

## Mission progress on the second code path

4.2.8 taught one of the two progress calculations to use the room your
robot is actually in. The other one — used once every room has a time
estimate — still worked purely from elapsed time. Robots that had
learned their room times ended up on the unfixed one.
