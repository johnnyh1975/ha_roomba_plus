# Roomba+ 4.3.1

**A 900-series' maps the right way round, areas that learn only from
missions started on the dock, and two maintenance fixes.**

[Upgrade notes →](../docs/UPGRADING.md#v431--from-v430)

---

## 900-series maps were mirror images of the floor

@liblit looked at the new area map in *Rooms & zones* for his Roomba 980
and said it plainly: the entire map is flipped left to right. A second
980 showed the same.

Roomba+ swaps the firmware's x and y before drawing a position, as the
robot's own client always did. A swap is a reflection, not a turn. In
the firmware's own frame the robot's heading and its direction of travel
agree; after the swap they only agree as 90° minus the heading. The data
from @liblit's 980 showed that on 94% of its steps, and his floor plan
fitted the recorded paths only when mirrored.

- **The cleaning path, the coverage map and the area map** are now drawn
  mirrored back, and show the floor the right way round. The heading
  arrow and user-drawn doors turn with them.
- **Nothing stored moves.** The coverage grid, the areas and their
  names, the doors, the trajectories and every position in millimetres
  (`map_x_mm`, `rooms`, the hazards) keep the frame they had; only the
  step to pixels mirrors. A path saved before the update is turned on
  restore.
- **Coverage map, for card authors:** on a 900-series a point now lands
  at `((x_max − x) · s, (y_max − y) · s)` of `render_extent_mm`.
- **Smart Map robots are drawn as before.** The mirror is confirmed on
  two 980s and nowhere else. If a Smart Map robot's cleaning path looks
  mirrored to you, say so.

## A part reset showed the robot's lifetime

On a j9+ (@msva17), after 4.3.0's maintenance fix, the filter, edge brush
and bag each read 1556 hours, as remaining time and as `max_hours`:
the robot's whole runtime.

Roomba+ learns a part's life from the hours between its resets. Before
4.3.0 those resets were recorded at 0 hours on robots that keep their
hours in `runtimeStats`. The first correct reset then made one interval
from 0 to 1556, and with two resets that one interval is the learned
life. An interval that starts at a reset recorded at 0 hours no longer
counts. It works on the history already stored, so nothing needs
pressing again.

## 900-series areas: hidden is hidden, one name is one room

From @liblit's questions about his 980's areas:

- **A hidden area was still recorded as cleaned** with each mission.
  Hiding is how an area that is not a real space gets put away
  (Configure → *Zone management*), so it no longer is.
- **Several areas with the same name are one room.** The area select and
  a mission's cleaned rooms list the name once. The areas stay separate
  on the map, and an area cannot be split.

## 900-series areas learn only from missions that started on the dock

Positions count from where a mission starts. A mission started away
from the dock lands turned and shifted against every other: @liblit's
980 on 27 August, its path turned by about 95°, 23% of its cells outside
the house, and the two phantom areas he found in *Rooms & zones*.

- **The robot says where it started:** the phase before the start.
  Charging means it was on the dock; any other phase means it started
  where it stood. iRobot's own record agrees: `dockedAtStart` was 0 for
  exactly that mission and 1 for the rest.
- **Such a mission is drawn, not learned from.** It stays out of the
  coverage grid, the areas and the doors. The diagnostics count it under
  `position_chain.missions_kept_out_of_grid`.
- **A start Roomba+ did not see** (Home Assistant started mid-mission)
  counts as on the dock, as every mission did before.

## Area names survive a recompute

When the areas are divided differently and a named area is absorbed by
another, its name now moves to the area that took most of it, unless
that one has a name of its own. Before, the name was deleted with the
old area and the new one asked to be named again.

## Doors measured at their widest, in the diagnostics

The width stored with each door is measured at the narrowest point of
the boundary, which is always where it meets the wall: 90–190 mm on
@liblit's 980, whatever the opening. Each door in the diagnostics now
also carries `opening_mm`, the opening at its widest, and
`constriction`, how much it narrows the floor. How areas are merged has
not changed; that waits until the measurement has been seen on more
than one house.

## Mission history: no half-minute "missions"

A run of under 30 seconds from a known start that cleaned nothing, with
nothing stuck or lifted, is no longer recorded as a completed mission.
@liblit's 980 had stored eleven of these among sixteen records, five
within five minutes one evening; iRobot's history has none of them.
Stored trajectories now carry their end time; `ended_at` was always
empty.

The same store held one mission twice: iRobot's mission 119, under two
ids, started 15 seconds apart and ended 12 seconds apart. Both
duplicate checks compared ids, so neither saw it, and the mission
counted twice in every statistic.

- **Two records are one mission when the start and the end each agree
  within two minutes.** The second only fills in what the first lacks,
  and its completion event is not fired again.
- **A store that already holds both is cleaned** when Home Assistant
  starts.
- **A 980's recharge segments stay apart.** They share the mission's
  start and end hours later, so the ends do not agree.
- Why it was recorded twice is not settled. Home Assistant restarted
  during that mission, which is the likeliest cause; the fold does not
  depend on it.

## Docs

`reset_robot_profile` was described as wiping the maintenance intervals.
It does not: a part's interval is learned from its own reset history,
which only a part reset changes. The feature guide and the action's
description now say what it does clear.

## Under the hood

- `map_mm_to_view()` in `map_renderer.py` is the one place the mirror
  happens; `RendererConfig.mirror_x` and `GridStore.mirror_x` switch it
  on, for 900-series robots only.
- `_learned_hours()` skips intervals that start at a 0-hour reset.
- `_mission_start_on_dock()` in `image.py` reads the phase before a
  start; the flag rides along in the mission checkpoint.
- `SegDoor` gains `opening_mm` and `constriction`, stored additively.
- `_same_mission()` in `mission_store.py` compares start and end
  (`SAME_MISSION_TOLERANCE_SEC = 120`); `async_append()` returns False
  for a folded record, and `async_record_mission()` stops there.
- **Tried and not shipped:** filling small enclosed holes before
  segmenting. It joined @liblit's fragmented kitchen and dining room,
  and on a second 980 merged six areas into two: there the holes are
  wall blocks between rooms, not furniture. An area-ratio check for
  missions was dropped too: it let the off-dock mission through.
