# Roomba+ 4.6.0b2

**The second beta of 4.6.0.** It needs *Include pre-releases* for Roomba+ in
the Marketplace (*Show beta versions* in HACS before Home Assistant 2026.11)
and is built on **roombapy-prime 0.9.1**, unchanged. Everything in
[4.6.0b1](v4.6.0b1.md) is in it, plus two fixes from @azeiop's report, one
for the blueprints, and documentation rewritten for 4.6.

Please report back:

- **A robot that showed *Possible layout change detected* since 4.5.3**
  (@azeiop): after the update, `binary_sensor.<robot>_layout_change_detected`
  should turn off and stay off after room cleans.
- **Missions sent out back to back with short top-ups in between**
  (@azeiop): *battery contact suspect* should not come back from them.
- Everything b1 asked for still stands; nothing of 4.6 has run on a robot
  yet.

---

## Layout spots from 4.5.3 are dropped once (@azeiop, #246)

4.5.4 stopped room cleans from counting as misses everywhere else, but the
spots collected before stayed in the coverage history: @azeiop's sensor
stayed on with 598 candidate cells. On the first start of this version
Roomba+ drops the current layout candidates from the history once, and
notes it in the log. The rest of the history is kept, and real changes
after that are detected as before.

## A short top-up is no battery trend (@azeiop, #246)

The battery-contact check compared the peaks of every charge, including
five-minute top-ups between missions that never reach full. Peaks of 100,
92, 89 and 87 % from those read as a battery losing contact. A charge now
counts once it reached full or lasted two hours.

## Blueprints: no file access on the event loop

b1 listed the integration's blueprint folder on the event loop after
copying, and Home Assistant logged a blocking call at every start. The
listing now happens with the copy, outside the loop. Found by the new
documentation demo, below.

## Documentation for 4.6

- **The README is a landing page**: what Roomba+ is, a screenshot, the
  supported robots and what works on which, how it compares with the
  built-in integration and roomba_rest980, installation from the
  Marketplace or HACS, setup and first steps.
- **New pages**: [V4/Prime robots](../docs/PRIME.md) and
  [Moving to Roomba+](../docs/MIGRATING.md). The device tracker, the known
  limitations and the planned ideas are in the
  [feature reference](../docs/FEATURES.md); how often data is fetched is in
  [data privacy](../docs/DATA_PRIVACY.md).
- **Screenshots**, from a demo Home Assistant with a simulated Roomba 980.
- **Checked against the code**, which corrected among others:
  - a 900-series robot cannot be sent to a room; the old table said it could;
  - the live map is `image.<robot>_map` (`_prime_cleaning_map` on Prime);
  - about ten entity ids, such as `sensor.<robot>_phase` for the status and
    `binary_sensor.<robot>_connected` on Prime;
  - the options are under *Connection settings* and *Maintenance*;
  - the cloud intervals in the data privacy page;
  - `roomba_plus.reset_battery` does not exist; it is a button.

## Under the hood

- `blueprint_install._copy()` returns the shipped names with the copied
  ones.
- `grid_store`: `COVERAGE_HISTORY_RULE` is stored with the grid; a grid
  without it drops its furniture candidates once on load.
- `repairs`: a charge peak counts after `_CHARGE_PEAK_MIN_SECONDS` (2 h) or
  at full; `current_charge_cycle_started` on the runtime data.
- `scripts/docs_demo/`: a simulated Roomba 980, its broker and the steps
  that regenerate the screenshots with one command.
