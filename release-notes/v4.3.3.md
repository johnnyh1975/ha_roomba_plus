## A room on another floor went out as a zone on this one

@catongates has an upstairs and a downstairs map. `clean_room: Bathroom`
(upstairs) went out on the downstairs map as zone 5, "In front of the
Oven": region ids are numbered per map, and the map and the type were
looked up by the bare id, which the downstairs zone had taken first. The
robot, upstairs, wandered through the office looking for it, and the
mission was recorded as the oven zone.

- **A room on a map the robot is not on is handed out with its map**,
  as `<map>/<id>`, and a command takes the map and the type from the
  id. Rooms on the current map keep their plain ids.
- **Rooms from two maps in one command are refused**, as Clean Area
  already did: the command names one map, and the other map's ids name
  other regions on it.

## "Name Smart Map Zones" for zones the account had named

@liblit restarted Home Assistant and was asked to name seven zones of
his i7, whose names come from his iRobot account. The account had not
answered yet at that start: when the first cloud fetch fails (a
network not up yet at boot is enough), the entry is set up without the
account's rooms, and the notice asked for names. The login retry ten
minutes later brought the names and changed neither.

- **With an iRobot account, the notice is never raised**, answered or
  not, and one left from an earlier start is taken down.
- **The entry is set up again once the cloud first answers** after a
  failed start, so the rooms, zones and selects are the account's
  without a manual reload.

## Smart Map robots: path, coverage and rooms map the same way round

@frnchfrgg tested every picture of his i-series (lewis firmware) against
the iRobot app, in 4.3.1 and 4.3.2, with and without a map rotation, and
@catongates sent the same three pictures from his i7+. Together they
settle what 4.3.2 got half right:

- **The path requested from the robot is in the cloud map's frame**, the
  frame of the rooms map and the cloud's coverage, not the dock's. Drawn
  through the axis swap made for the robot's own position, it came out a
  reflection of the rooms map (4.3.1) or a quarter turn off it (4.3.2).
  It is now drawn as the rooms map draws the cloud map. On
  @frnchfrgg's robot it only seemed to follow the app's turn in 4.3.2:
  he had turned the app's map by exactly that quarter.
- **The coverage map is no longer mirrored.** On these robots it is
  filled from the cloud's coverage, in the cloud map's frame; 4.3.2
  mirrored it with the path, and it came out flipped where 4.3.1 had it
  right.
- **Map rotation turns the rooms map too**, and the pixel positions it
  publishes for cards turn with it. It turned the path and the coverage
  and left the rooms map as it was.

All three pictures start in the map's own orientation, the one it had
right after mapping. To match a map turned in the iRobot app, set *Map
rotation*: on @frnchfrgg's robot the app's quarter turn counter-clockwise
is 270°. His diagnostics show the app's turn too: the map's
`user_orientation_rad` dropped by exactly a quarter (4.7163 → 3.1454)
when he turned it a quarter counter-clockwise, `robot_orientation_rad`
being the orientation right after mapping. Following the app on its own
is one more robot's diagnostics away.

A Smart Map robot that publishes its own position has not been checked
against its floor yet; its path is drawn as in 4.3.2.

The diagnostics gain what the next such question needs:

- **`umf_poses`**: the headings of the cloud map's start, end and dock
  poses and their distances from the map's origin, never where they
  are, beside the map's angles (`pmap_versions` → `orientation_rad`).
- **`position_chain` → `aligner_confidence`**: how close the aligner
  came (aligned from 0.70; 0.0 when it never had two pairs to match),
  and **`aligner_bootstrap_markers`**: how many markers it had from the
  cloud's room crossings. A robot that publishes no position never has
  the markers the aligner matches against; the ones from room crossings
  are in the cloud map's frame already.

## A mission that ended in an error counted twice

The maintainer's 980 stored 15 of its last 79 missions twice: once as
the robot wrote it, once taken from iRobot's cloud. A 980 mission that
ends in an error (17, 2, 4) is closed by the cloud at the error, 54
minutes in, while the robot reports it over hours later. The two
records start together and end apart, and the pairing with the cloud
compared only the ends, so the cloud's copy was recorded as a mission
of its own. Every such error was counted twice, and the robot's own
record never got the area the cloud had for it.

- **One record per mission.** The cloud's copy is recognised by its
  start and folded into the robot's record, which keeps its own times
  and result and takes the area and the cloud's other fields. Copies
  already stored are folded when Home Assistant starts.
- **Missions Home Assistant missed are still taken from the cloud.** On
  the same 980, eight of them.
- **A 980's recharge segments share one start**, so the cloud's fields
  go to a segment only by its end, never to all of them.

## Under the hood

- `_cloud_twin()` and `_local_has_cloud_twin()` in `mission_store.py`
  pair a cloud copy with the robot's own record by start (≤ 120 s, the
  cloud's end no later than the robot's). `_without_same_mission_twice()`,
  `async_append()`, `adopt_missing_from_cloud()`, `backfill_from_cloud()`
  and `merge_latest_from_cloud()` use it; `_cloud_twin_of()` refuses an
  ambiguous pairing.
- `RoombaMapImage._handle_live_position()` hands requested positions to
  the renderer in the cloud map's frame (pre-mirrored where the renderer
  mirrors, heading less a quarter); `GridStore.mirror_x` is set for the
  900-series only; `RoombaRoomsImage` turns its pixels by the map
  rotation (`_turn_px`, cache key and `_to_px_last` included).
- `ClassicRoomCleaning.available_rooms()` hands out rooms from other
  maps as `<map>/<id>`; `clean_rooms()` reads map and type per position
  and refuses two maps (`rooms_different_floors`). An alias stored
  against a bare id resolves to the id handed out for it.
- `SmartZoneSelect` suppresses the naming notice whenever a cloud
  coordinator exists and removes a stale one at start;
  `_reload_once_the_cloud_answers()` reloads the entry on the first
  successful cloud refresh after a failed initial fetch.
- **Start time:** the load-time check for one mission stored twice
  compared every record with every other, on the event loop: about a
  quarter of a second for a full history of 365 missions at each start
  of Home Assistant since 4.3.1. It compares neighbours in time now,
  under 2 ms.
- `umf_typed_poses()` keeps the UMF's dock, start and end poses and its
  header angles in the fetched UMF (`poses`); `umf_pose_report()` puts
  their headings and distances from the origin, no positions, into the
  diagnostics as `umf_poses`.
- **The second 980's test data** moved to `tests/fixtures/tester_980/`,
  and the tester's handle left the code, the tests and the docs, at his
  request. The test classes are `TestTheSecond980sHome` and
  `TestTheSecond980sWholeStore`.
- **The maintainer's mission store of 9 October** joins the fixtures,
  room names replaced (`roomba_plus_missions_02_…`), for
  `TestTheCloudsCopyOfAnErrorMission`.
