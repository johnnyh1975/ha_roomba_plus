# Roomba+ 4.5.0b6

**The sixth beta of 4.5.0.** Everything in 4.5.0 is listed in
[4.5.0](v4.5.0.md); this page is what changed since
[4.5.0b5](v4.5.0b5.md).

Needs *Show beta versions* in HACS. Built on **roombapy-prime 0.8.2**,
as b4 and b5.

---

## Fixed

- **The rooms under the cleaning path, and the room map, empty after a
  mission** (@pk-1966, i7+, #211). No restart, no update, and the next
  look at the map showed the path on a blank background. His
  diagnostics showed why: after the mission the iRobot cloud made a new
  map version the active one, marked `creator: "cloud"` and identical to
  what the robot had uploaded. That version has no rooms. Roomba+ read
  the room outlines from it; your rooms, your schedule and the room
  commands are on your own version.
  - Roomba+ now passes over such a version as it already did for one
    the robot made itself, and reads the rooms from your version.
  - The same choice gives the map version for a room command when
    neither the app nor a favourite has stored one.
  - 4.5.0b5 named a map that failed to load at startup as the likely
    cause. That was not it for him; the retry it added stays.

- **Prime map: the inner edge of a border area was not drawn**
  (@Rickeeh, PR #223, Combo 105). Borders are areas, drawn as outlines
  so they do not cover the rooms; an area with a hole showed only its
  outer edge. Both edges are drawn now. Room outlines, room tracking and
  cleaning are unchanged. Whether this was the wall missing on his map
  is not established.

## New

- **SmartScrub per room on Prime** (@gunzxz, Plus 405 Combo):
  `smart_scrub` in `clean_room` goes out as `swScrub` in each room's
  parameters, 1 on and 0 off, nothing for a room you leave empty. It was
  accepted and dropped before. `pad_wetness` stays Classic-only. There
  is no household SmartScrub switch: these robots report SmartScrub per
  room, and what the household value does beside each room's own
  setting is not known. Not yet confirmed on a robot.
